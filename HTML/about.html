    HISTORY

    Lee Hong Engineering Works was founded in 1991 in Pusing, Perak, with a vision to provide reliable and professional heavy vehicle solutions. What started as a small workshop has grown into a trusted name in lorry repair, installation, and rental services.

    For over three decades, we have built our reputation on quality workmanship, integrity, and long-term client relationships. Our dedicated team combines technical expertise with hands-on service to ensure every lorry is safe, dependable, and road-ready.

    Today, Lee Hong Engineering Works continues to serve businesses and drivers across the region, staying true to our commitment of keeping heavy vehicles moving with confidence and care.
